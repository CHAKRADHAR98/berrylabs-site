We build search for the things that are hard to find.

The result you need is usually the rarest one: one collaborator in a crowd of hundreds, one candidate in a market of millions. Search tools are tuned for what is popular and frequent, so they miss what is rare and specific. They also expect you to know the right keywords before you start, which is exactly what you don't know when you're looking for someone new.

We build intelligent sparse search systems and matchmaking algorithms. You describe what you're after in your own words. Our systems work out the intent behind it, weigh what each side wants, and bring back the few matches worth your time.

Today it works at events. Walk into a room of 300 people and you get the four you should talk to first, each with a reason to say hello. Around 400 people have used it in pilots across three large events.

Next is hiring, where the right person is just as rare and far more costly to miss.
